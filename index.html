Why the library exists

What we learn in training deserves a home.

Seminar notes end up in chats, good worksheets get lost in downloads folders, and insights from supervision are never written down. The library gathers all of it in one calm place – searchable, in our languages, and written by the people who use it.

Knowledge that stays

Every paper, worksheet and set of notes has a headline, a short summary, tags and an author – so it can be found again years later.

Learning over time

Understanding grows. Authors add dated updates instead of quietly rewriting, so everyone can see what changed and why.

Across languages

The library works in Ukrainian and English. Each post shows whether a translation exists or is still needed – an open invitation to help.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who can join the library?

Members of the EFT community. You sign in with your Google account; access is granted once your account has been approved.

Is anything public?

No. The library is not visible to search engines or visitors. Only signed-in members can read posts, comments and attachments.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in Ukrainian and English. Posts are written in the author’s language and can be marked as translated or in need of translation.

Can I change or remove a post later?

Yes. You can edit your posts, add dated updates, take them offline at any time or delete them permanently from your dashboard.

What about client confidentiality?

Case material must always be anonymised before it is shared. If you notice identifying details in a post, tell the author or the team so it can be corrected.
